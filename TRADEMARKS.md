# The Shaderera name and logo

The code is [MIT](LICENSE): use it, change it and ship it in anything, including products you
sell. The MIT licence covers the code. It does not cover the name "Shaderera" or its logo, which
identify the official project.

You may:
- say that your product, film or service uses, works with or is built on Shaderera;
- publish a fork, under another name, saying it is not the official project;
- use the name in articles, talks, tutorials and comparisons.

Please do not, without asking first:
- name a product, package, service or domain "Shaderera", or something easy to confuse with it;
- use the logo, or suggest that the official project made or endorses your product.

To ask, open an [issue](https://github.com/shaderera/shaderera/issues).
