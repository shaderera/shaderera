# 0010: The engine's licence, and a fresh history in public

2026-10-10. Owner accepted. Its licence part is superseded by
[0013](0013-the-engine-is-mit.md): from 0.1.1 the engine is MIT.

**Licence.** Decision 0008 said companies pay for the engine, as with Remotion, while every
package said MIT. Code published under MIT stays MIT, so this was settled before anything went
public:
- **The engine** (`@shaderera/core`, `shaderera`, `@shaderera/registry`) is under the Shaderera
  License (`LICENSE`). It is free for individuals, for-profit organisations of up to 3 people,
  non-profits and education, and for a 30-day evaluation. Other companies need a company
  license. Whatever a user makes with it is theirs.
- **What lands in a user's project is MIT** (`LICENSE-MIT`): `create-shaderera`, the registry
  items in `packages/catalog/` (fonts keep OFL 1.1), and the agent skill. A user can change and share
  their own film's code without a licence question.
- The licence text is a draft. 2026-10-12: published as a draft at 0.1.0; a lawyer reviews it
  later, and the threshold (3 people) and the terms of a company license may change then.

**History.** The private repository's history holds working documents that were removed from the
tree. The public repository starts at 0.1.0 from a single commit of the cleaned tree. The private
repository stays the archive.
